Good to know.

Who is invited?

Women and trans women, 18 and over, from all walks of life, backgrounds and experiences.

What is a Soulcial?

Think early 2000s youth club, but for adults, rooted in creativity, community, knowledge and wellness. Each one brings together special guests and creative sessions to inspire, fulfil and recharge you.

What does social battery friendly mean?

There is no pressure to socialise in any particular way. Sit quietly and get stuck into something creative, have a deep chat or drift between the two. It is designed with introverts, extroverts and introverted extroverts in mind.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Show up exactly as you are, on your own or with a friend.

Do I need to be creative?

No. The sessions are led by people who know their stuff and you can join in at your own pace.
